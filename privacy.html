Doodoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Short version: Doodoku collects nothing. Nobody's watching you go.

What we collect

Nothing. Doodoku has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. The only time it goes online is to ask Apple's App Store about the Royal Flush purchase.

Purchases

The Royal Flush is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account; we never see your name, email, card or any other payment details. The app only learns whether this Apple Account owns the Royal Flush, and keeps no record of that itself. Apple's own privacy policy covers the purchase.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels cleared, best times, stars, your Daily Dump streak, any puzzle in progress) and your settings are saved in a file on your iPhone. That file never leaves your device. We can't see it, and no one else can either.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset progress in the app, or delete the app. Either one removes your progress for good.

Children

Doodoku collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above before the app does anything different.
