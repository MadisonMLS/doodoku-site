Doodoodoku Support

The puzzle you play while you go.

How do I play?

Put exactly one poo in every row, every column and every tile pattern. Poos can't touch, not even diagonally. Tap a square once for toilet paper (your "not here" mark), twice for a poo, three times to clear it. Potty Training in Settings walks you through it.

I'm stuck.

Tap the Plunger. It finds the next move you can make by logic and tells you why. Every puzzle has exactly one answer and never needs a guess.

I bought the Royal Flush but the bathrooms are locked.

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. If someone in your Family Sharing group bought it, it should appear on your device too; Restore Purchases also picks that up.

Refunds

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

My progress disappeared.

Progress lives only on your iPhone, so deleting the app deletes it. The Royal Flush itself is never lost: Restore Purchases brings it back.

Are there really no ads?

Really. The Remove Ads button is a joke. Doodoodoku has no ads and collects no data. See the privacy policy.
